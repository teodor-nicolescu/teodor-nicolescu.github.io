My story

Connecting theory and practice.

My academic journey began at the Faculty of Medical Engineering at POLITEHNICA Bucharest, where I earned a bachelor's degree in Medical Systems and Equipment and a master's degree in Modern Technologies for Medical Engineering. I am currently pursuing a PhD at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, focusing on artificial intelligence-based systems for human behavior assessment, particularly the detection of concealed and deceptive behavior through physiological signal analysis.

My research and development work has involved acquiring, preprocessing, and analyzing multimodal physiological signals, building and augmenting datasets, and developing, training, and optimizing machine-learning models for signal classification and emotion recognition. These experiences have strengthened my interest in computational methods for interpreting behavioral and psychophysiological responses.

Alongside research, I have gained extensive experience in IT system administration and completed professional training in Java backend and full-stack development. Combining engineering, research, and software development allows me to approach technical challenges from both scientific and practical perspectives.
